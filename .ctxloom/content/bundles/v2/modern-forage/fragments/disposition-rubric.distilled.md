# disposition-rubric

Each candidate gets exactly one classification + explicit reason.

## ✅ PROMOTE
- Meets 2+ of 4 selection criteria
- 5+ named sources verified
- Multi-vendor OR single-vendor w/ structural lock (apparatus/operational routine/commitment purchase)
- Diaspora signal documented OR strong containment evidence (media invisibility, format complexity)

## ◧ PROMOTE WITH CONTAINMENT CAVEAT
- Structurally interesting; dish escaped metro
- Practice/prep/scene locked (Flaming Saganaki pattern)
- "Two places, no national attention" (Walker Bros pattern)
- Include marginalia blockquote explaining caveat

## ⏸ DEFER
- Under-sourced (1-2 sources)
- Needs additional research (cookbook mining, oral history, diaspora threads)
- Note: what would unlock promotion?

## ❌ EXCLUDE
Always with explicit reason:
- **Too nationally known** — national chain adoption (e.g., deep-dish)
- **Single restaurant, not regional tradition** — e.g., Shady Glen cheese-skirt burger
- **Ingredient not dish** — e.g., Montana huckleberries
- **Format not dish** — e.g., Hawaii plate lunch
- **Ecosystem not adaptation** — e.g., LA Koreatown KBBQ
- **Origin heavily contested** — e.g., chimichanga
- **Brand substituting for dish** — Grippo's, Cheerwine, Vernors (use regional-condiments/snacks track)
