# Modern Forage Containment Criteria

A candidate qualifies for the survey if it meets at least TWO of:

1. **Geographic containment** — rarely found more than ~100-200 miles from origin
2. **Name-recognition failure** — ordering it elsewhere produces a blank stare
3. **No national chain adoption** — hasn't been picked up by national franchises
4. **Active local culture** — not a museum piece; locals eat it constantly

Nationally famous regional icons (deep-dish pizza, Philly cheesesteaks, Nashville hot chicken, lobster rolls, fluffernutter) are out — they've already escaped.

## Source threshold

- **Minimum**: 2 independent named sources for inclusion at all
- **Target**: 5-8 named sources for confident promotion to canonical doc
- "Named" means food media, local TV, longform journalism, academic press, podcast transcripts, oral histories — NOT just Reddit comments or single user reviews
- Reddit threads count as a *seed signal*, not a source

## Containment-mechanism taxonomy

When a candidate passes, identify which mechanism(s) lock it. From the tracker:

1. **Immigrant-community-locked** — Greek diner empire, Chinese-American adaptations, Cornish mining corridor, German-Russian Great Plains. Most common.
2. **Apparatus-locked** — Chicago aquarium smoker, Provel cheese, Old Vienna chip seasoning machinery.
3. **Operational-routine-locked** — Pizza Pot Pie's dough-and-bowl flip, Brown Hotel's Hot Brown service.
4. **Commitment-purchase-locked** — Atomic Cake (whole cakes only), Pittsburgh Cookie Table (wedding-only).

## Geographic ladder

- **Hyperlocal** — locked to one neighborhood, one shop, or one immigrant enclave (Pizza Pot Pie at 2121 N Clark; mild sauce in Bronzeville)
- **City-wide** — across the metro, multiple vendors, multiple neighborhoods (Goetta, Italian Beef)
- **Regional** — cross-metro within a coherent geography (UP Pasty across Marquette/Houghton/Iron Mountain; Beef Roll across the Illinois Valley)

A hyperlocal entry can be **promoted to regional** when independent threads or research surface the same dish across adjacent metros.

## Allowed partial-containment readings

- **"Two places, no national attention"** — Walker Bros Apple Pancake (Portland origin → Chicago North Shore home, neither nationally known). Qualifies despite multiple legitimate homes.
- **"Practice-locked but dish-traveled"** — Flaming Saganaki (dish escaped to Greek restaurants nationwide, but the flame and "Opa!" theater stays Chicago-locked). Include with explicit caveat blockquote.
- **"Total media invisibility"** — Beef Roll (dozens of menus, no Wikipedia / no NYT / no LTHForum). Broad local presence + total media silence is itself the strongest containment signal.

## Diaspora signal

The #1 lead generator per the tracker. Comments shaped like "I moved from X and can't find Y anywhere" are the strongest seed-quality input. Treat them as primary, then validate with named sources.
