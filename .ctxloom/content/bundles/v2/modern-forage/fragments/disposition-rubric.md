# Disposition Rubric

Each candidate that passes initial filtering gets exactly one of these classifications, with an explicit reason.

## ✅ PROMOTE
- Meets 2+ of the 4 selection criteria
- 5+ named sources verified
- Multi-vendor presence (or single-vendor with explicit structural lock — apparatus, operational routine, or commitment purchase)
- Diaspora signal documented OR strong containment evidence (media invisibility, format complexity)

## ◧ PROMOTE WITH CONTAINMENT CAVEAT
- Structurally interesting but the dish has escaped the metro
- Practice/preparation/scene is locked even if the dish isn't (Flaming Saganaki pattern)
- "Two places, no national attention" reading (Walker Bros pattern)
- Mark as marginalia in the entry; include a blockquote explaining the caveat

## ⏸ DEFER
- Promising but under-sourced (1-2 sources only)
- Needs additional research mechanisms (cookbook mining, oral history, focused diaspora threads)
- Note specifically: what would unlock promotion?

## ❌ EXCLUDE
Always with an explicit reason from this list:
- **Too nationally known** — has national chain adoption (deep-dish, Philly cheesesteak, lobster rolls, fluffernutter, Brunswick stew, Key lime pie)
- **Single restaurant, not regional tradition** — Shady Glen cheese-skirt burger, Johnny's Cafe steaks, Fry Bread House (single restaurant ≠ city tradition; the dish must exist beyond one room)
- **Ingredient not dish** — Montana huckleberries, brand-only items
- **Format not dish** — Hawaii plate lunch, Vegas casino food, "school lunch culture"
- **Ecosystem not adaptation** — LA Koreatown KBBQ, Stockton Filipino food (authentic immigrant food rather than locally-created adaptation)
- **Origin contested too heavily** — chimichanga (Tucson vs Yuma vs Mexico)
- **Brand substituting for dish** — Grippo's, Cheerwine, Vernors. Brands belong in the regional-condiments / regional-snacks tracks, not the main dish survey.

## ↑ PROMOTION CANDIDATE (hyperlocal → regional)
- Existing canonical entry currently city-locked
- This thread (or independently surfaced research) shows it in another adjacent metro
- Recommend reframing as regional or adding cross-reference to the existing entry

## JBF America's Classics caveat
JBF Classics honor *restaurants*, not *dishes*. A JBF win is a useful lead generator but needs a containment filter: does the *dish* exist beyond one restaurant? If no, it's an institution, not a regional food. EXCLUDE under "single restaurant, not regional tradition" with the JBF win noted.
