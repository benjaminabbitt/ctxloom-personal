# Sentence Structure

Structural tells outlive word lists (words get patched per model generation; templates persist).

## Banned patterns
- Rule-of-three triads ("clear, concise, and compelling") — one precise item beats three vague; four things → list four
- Antithesis: "not just X, but Y" / "It's not X — it's Y" → state Y
- Trailing participial analysis: fact + ", highlighting/ensuring/underscoring..." → own sentence with a real argument, or delete
- Staccato triplets: "No X. No Y. Just Z."
- Hedging stacks ("may potentially", "could possibly") — one modal max, real uncertainty only
- False ranges ("from startups to enterprises")
- Vague attribution ("studies show", "experts note") — cite the real source or drop; never invent one
- Certainty inflation ("undoubtedly", "clearly", "obviously") on contestable claims

## Rhythm
- Vary sentence length; occasional fragment fine
- Words proportional to importance; equal-length paragraphs = machine signature
- Em dashes ≤1 per paragraph; prefer comma, colon, parens, new sentence. Density is the tell
